**Why:** The work spans many sessions and long runs. Without this, a new session re-derives decisions and re-runs hours of experiments.
**How to apply:** When you touch `beamroot/models/cascade.py`, cascade growth in `beamroot/growth.py`, or the `cascade`, `cascade29`, `cascadeinit` or `cascadefreeze` experiment grids. Delete this entry once the decisions below are made and recorded in an ADR.

State on 2026-10-31 (code committed through `9665737`; experiment [0004](../experiments/0004-cascade-sizing.md), status pending user review):

- **Built:** `--model cascade` with sized node models (ADR 0005), growth by cluster splits, size checks and bumps, pruned soft routing, `--leaf {classifier,label}`, `evaluate` MACs and ms per image, cascade-aware report and class flow, the grids above, and the flags `--leaf-init {fresh,copy}`, `--settle-epochs`, `--bump-cooldown`. Defaults reproduce earlier behaviour (not diffed bit-for-bit). Router-freeze work (uncommitted on top of `cc56dd3`): per-node Adam groups, `--router-lr-decay D`, `--freeze-routers {off,quality}` with `--freeze-entropy-max`, `trained_node_evals`/`freezes` in metrics.jsonl and the summary, and grid `cascadefreeze`. With both flags off, training matches the single-group optimiser (`test_node_param_groups_train_like_one_group`).
- **Findings (MNIST, seed 0 only):** `dag` is 0.991 at 4.76M beam-1 MACs. Label leaves lost badly (0.59–0.77 at 19 nodes, 0.68–0.82 at 29 nodes, stuck near 13 leaves). `hidden`-axis cascade matches `dag` accuracy at 2.5× the MACs. `--leaf-init copy` adds about 2.5 points to the cheap `resolution` and `both` variants, and settle 3 plus cooldown 2 cuts bumps (18 to 12 or fewer) and more than halves MACs. Best cost point: `both-classifier-copy-settle`, 0.958 at 1.35M MACs (3.5× cheaper than `dag`, 3.3 points less accurate).
- **User decisions so far:** CIFAR-10 is skipped for now. Label leaves should be dropped (they are poor); their code and `--leaf label` flag stay until the user says delete, and the loss is written up in entry 0004. Everything else is undecided.
- **Not yet done:** `copy`/`settle` on the `hidden` axis; separating settle from cooldown; the finish steps of the feature plan (end-to-end verification and retrospective).
- **Finished (2026-11-01):** grid `cascadefreeze`, experiment [0005](../experiments/0005-router-freeze.md), results in `runs/experiments/cascadefreeze-mnist/summary.{json,md}` (11 h for 10 runs). Freeze and decay are not adopted (flags stay, default off): `-freeze` ties on beam-1 but loses 0.10 leaf purity; `-decay-freeze` ties on both. Over three seeds `both-classifier-copy-settle` is 3.0 points below `dag` (significant) at about 1.8× fewer MACs, not 3.5×. Next: decide the defaults for `copy`, `settle` and `cooldown` with the user.
- **Training-time probe (2026-10-31):** re-ran `both-classifier` fresh and settle for 4 epochs. The settle graph was identical (same node evals as the grid run), yet epoch 4 took 163 s against 299 s in the grid, and fresh took 126 s against 176 s. So wall clock on this machine (2019 Intel MacBook Pro, AMD 5300M with 4 GB VRAM) varies 1.4–1.8× from one day to the next for the same work. MPS driver memory levels off at about 440 MB, there is no swap and no CPU throttling, so memory is not the cause. Treat `train min` as noise between runs; compare a deterministic cost (node evals per image) instead.
- **Design points to remember:** a step-1 node pools once, not twice, at resolution 8 or below (`SMALL_RES`), otherwise it has 16 features; label-leaf logits start at N(0, 0.5²) because uniform leaves give routers zero gradient; cascade variants must pass `--start-step 1` or no size check can ever bump (default step 4 is the maximum).
